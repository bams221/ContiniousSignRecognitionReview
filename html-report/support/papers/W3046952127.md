# Sign Language Recognition: A Deep Survey

A broad survey of visual deep-learning approaches to isolated and continuous sign recognition, covering input modalities, datasets, methods and open challenges.

**Proposed eligible review (full text).** Meets M1–M3 as a broader review with qualifying CSLR coverage. Section 7 and Table 8 (pp.18–20) compare Pu et al. (2018) and Cui et al. (2019) by methods, datasets and reported error rates, meeting the project-specific two-study threshold. [Evidence](../evidence/W3046952127.json).

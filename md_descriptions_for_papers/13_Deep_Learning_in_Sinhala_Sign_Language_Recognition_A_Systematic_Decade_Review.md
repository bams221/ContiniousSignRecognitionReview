**3. Brief synthesis**

- **Main emphasis:** A short, Sinhala-focused overview of methods and resources, with useful discussion of limited public datasets, weak validation, and deployment constraints.
- **Coverage limitations:** CSLR appears through a small number of brief examples and future-work statements. The paper offers no substantive CSLR architecture, alignment, training, or benchmark synthesis; consequently, none of the fixed thematic categories merits **2**.
- **Verification needed:** The text associates manual components with isolated signs and non-manual components with continuous signs. This conflates **cue type** with **recognition task** and should not be used as your screening definition. [PDF p. 3, §III.A] Likewise, the reported **12.70 WER** lacks enough dataset/protocol information here for reliable cross-study comparison. [PDF p. 4, §IV.D]

For your comparison, classify it as a **Sinhala-focused SLR review with limited CSLR coverage**, rather than a dedicated or technically comprehensive CSLR survey.

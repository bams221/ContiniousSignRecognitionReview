**3. Brief synthesis**

- **Main emphasis:** A broad survey of deep visual methods relevant to signing, with substantial space devoted to hand and human-pose tasks. CSLR receives meaningful coverage of early deep architectures, segmentation, and alignment, but is not the organizing focus of the whole review.
- **Coverage limitations:** CSLR-specific treatment of acquisition, cue fusion, training strategies, and language modeling is limited or ambiguous. Augmentation and generative models are covered elsewhere in the paper, but cannot be credited as CSLR coverage.
- **Verification needed:** The paper contains reporting inconsistencies. Mocialov et al. receives **95% accuracy** in §7.1 but **80.70%** in Table 8; SubUNets receives **40.7 WER** in §7.2 but **40.8** in Table 8. Its SubUNets modality/dataset attribution also needs checking before reuse. [PDF pp. 19–20, §§7.1–7.2, Table 8]

For your comparison, classify this as a **broad SLR/deep-vision survey with substantive secondary CSLR coverage**. Its limitations alone do not establish that a new CSLR review is necessary.
